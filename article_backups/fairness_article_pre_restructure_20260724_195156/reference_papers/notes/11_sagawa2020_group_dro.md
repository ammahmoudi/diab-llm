## Why It Matters

Foundational worst-group robustness work supporting the claim that optimization and regularization can change subgroup outcomes.

## Evidence Scope

This note records claims at the scope supported by the locally archived source or its verified metadata. It is a literature-tracking record, not a source of quantitative claims beyond the cited paper.

## Research Question

Why distributionally robust objectives can fail without appropriate regularization under group shifts.

## Method and Evidence

Studies group distributional robustness across benchmark shifts and analyzes the role of regularization.

## Findings Relevant Here

Shows that subgroup-oriented training behavior is sensitive to objective and regularization choices.

## Limitations and Claim Boundary

Worst-group accuracy is not equal opportunity, and benchmark group shifts are not the OhioT1DM protocol.

## Relevance to This Article

Keep to motivate that subgroup outcomes require empirical evaluation rather than assumptions about robust training.
