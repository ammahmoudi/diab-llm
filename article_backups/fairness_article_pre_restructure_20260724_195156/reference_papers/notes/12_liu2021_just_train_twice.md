## Why It Matters

A group-robustness approach without group labels, useful as a contrast to this paper's group-aware interventions.

## Evidence Scope

## Research Question

Whether repeated training can identify and improve hard-group performance without explicit group annotations.

## Method and Evidence

Uses error-based identification of difficult examples followed by retraining.

## Findings Relevant Here

Shows one route to group robustness without demographic labels.

## Limitations and Claim Boundary

Its assumptions and worst-group objective differ from clinically defined hypoglycemia TPR parity.
