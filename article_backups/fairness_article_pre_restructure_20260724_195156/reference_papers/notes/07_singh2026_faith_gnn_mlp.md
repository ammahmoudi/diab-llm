## Why It Matters

A graph-learning fair-distillation example that is outside the present clinical sequence-model scope.

## Evidence Scope

This note records claims at the scope supported by the locally archived source or its verified metadata. It is a literature-tracking record, not a source of quantitative claims beyond the cited paper.

## Research Question

Whether a graph neural network can transfer predictive and fairness properties to an MLP student.

## Method and Evidence

Distills graph-derived knowledge to a non-graph student while evaluating fairness-aware inference.

## Findings Relevant Here

Provides evidence that architecture changes during distillation can affect fairness outcomes.

## Limitations and Claim Boundary

Graph tasks, data topology, and fairness metrics do not transfer directly to Time-LLM forecasting or ECG classification.
