## Why It Matters

A benchmark for stereotypical bias in question answering; it is not needed for the final clinical Time-LLM argument.

## Evidence Scope

This note records claims at the scope supported by the locally archived source or its verified metadata. It is a literature-tracking record, not a source of quantitative claims beyond the cited paper.

## Research Question

How to measure social stereotypes in question-answering systems.

## Method and Evidence

Introduces a hand-built QA benchmark with bias-sensitive contexts and evaluation measures.

## Findings Relevant Here

Provides a standard example of an LLM social-bias benchmark.

## Limitations and Claim Boundary

It evaluates language understanding rather than clinical prediction, KD, or event-level fairness.
