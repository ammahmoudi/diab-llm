## Research Question

Whether glucose prediction should be assessed with clinically relevant criteria beyond conventional accuracy metrics.

## Method and Evidence

Develops and evaluates an event-aware composite perspective combining prediction error, temporal behavior, and glycemic-event performance.

## Findings Relevant Here

Shows that aggregate accuracy can favor forecasts that miss clinically important glycemic events.

## Limitations and Claim Boundary

It does not evaluate demographic fairness or the present Time-LLM/KD models.
