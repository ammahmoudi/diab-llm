## Why It Matters

Documents incomplete demographic reporting in public biosignal resources, a direct limitation for fairness claims using MIT-BIH-like datasets.

## Evidence Scope

## Research Question

How consistently demographic information is reported across PhysioNet biosignal datasets.

## Method and Evidence

Systematic analysis of demographic reporting in open PhysioNet resources.

## Findings Relevant Here

Finds incomplete demographic reporting, limiting subgroup evaluation and generalizability claims.
