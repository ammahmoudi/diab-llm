## Why It Matters

Documents inequities in CGM access, providing context but not evidence of algorithmic bias in glucose forecasting.

## Evidence Scope

This note records claims at the scope supported by the locally archived source or its verified metadata. It is a literature-tracking record, not a source of quantitative claims beyond the cited paper.

## Research Question

Whether CGM use differs across demographic and socioeconomic groups among women with type 1 diabetes.

## Method and Evidence

Observational analysis of T1D Exchange data on CGM use and participant characteristics.

## Findings Relevant Here

Reports disparities in CGM use by race/ethnicity and socioeconomic indicators within the studied population.

## Limitations and Claim Boundary

Access disparities do not prove predictive-model bias, and the population is not OhioT1DM.
