## Why It Matters

Demonstrates fairness-aware KD under biased, data-free conditions; it is a methodological contrast to the observed-data clinical setting.

## Evidence Scope

This note records claims at the scope supported by the locally archived source or its verified metadata. It is a literature-tracking record, not a source of quantitative claims beyond the cited paper.

## Research Question

How to reduce bias when data-free KD is trained from a biased teacher without original training data.

## Method and Evidence

Uses adversarial or constrained optimization in data-free KD to control fairness-related behavior.

## Findings Relevant Here

Shows that KD fairness interventions can be formulated as constrained optimization, but in a different data-access regime.

## Limitations and Claim Boundary

Data-free classification and its fairness objective differ from the present patient-level clinical forecasting problem.

## Relevance to This Article

Keep as a brief example of heterogeneous fair-KD methods; do not compare metric values.
