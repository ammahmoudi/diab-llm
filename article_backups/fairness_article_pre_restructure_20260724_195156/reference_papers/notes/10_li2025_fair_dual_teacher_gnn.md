## Why It Matters

A dual-teacher GNN method that is conceptually related to teacher-side interventions but not needed for the final clinical narrative.

## Evidence Scope

This note records claims at the scope supported by the locally archived source or its verified metadata. It is a literature-tracking record, not a source of quantitative claims beyond the cited paper.

## Research Question

Whether dual teachers can improve fairness in graph neural-network distillation.

## Method and Evidence

Uses multiple teachers to guide a graph student with fairness-aware objectives.

## Findings Relevant Here

Illustrates teacher-selection and multi-teacher strategies as one family of fair-KD interventions.

## Limitations and Claim Boundary

Graph learning and multi-teacher assumptions are absent from the present fixed single-teacher protocol.
