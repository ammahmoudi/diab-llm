## Research Question

How to preserve fairness during logit-based KD when new classes arrive sequentially.

## Method and Evidence

Uses logit distillation with fairness-aware handling of class-incremental updates.

## Findings Relevant Here

Shows that fairness can drift during sequential KD and requires explicit monitoring.

## Limitations and Claim Boundary
