## Why It Matters

One of the closest direct analyses of how KD changes group, individual, and class fairness across teacher and student models.

## Evidence Scope

This note records claims at the scope supported by the locally archived source or its verified metadata. It is a literature-tracking record, not a source of quantitative claims beyond the cited paper.

## Research Question

How knowledge transfer affects multiple fairness notions and whether effects depend on distillation choices.

## Method and Evidence

Compares teacher, distilled student, and independently trained student models across fairness measures and KD settings.

## Findings Relevant Here

Finds that KD can alter fairness rather than simply preserve the teacher's fairness, and that the direction of change depends on the setting.

## Limitations and Claim Boundary

The task, datasets, groups, and fairness definitions are not the OhioT1DM event-level EO protocol.

## Relevance to This Article

Keep as the strongest general citation for the claim that average utility retention does not establish fairness preservation under KD.
