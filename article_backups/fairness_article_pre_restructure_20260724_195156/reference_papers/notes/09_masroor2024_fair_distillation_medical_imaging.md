Medical-imaging fair-distillation work that establishes clinical relevance while remaining task and modality distinct.

## Evidence Scope

This note records claims at the scope supported by the locally archived source or its verified metadata. It is a literature-tracking record, not a source of quantitative claims beyond the cited paper.

## Research Question

Whether a student can learn fair behavior from a biased teacher in medical imaging.

## Method and Evidence

Uses a fairness-oriented distillation design in a medical-imaging classification setting.

## Findings Relevant Here

Supports the feasibility of fairness-aware KD in clinical ML and the need to compare utility with subgroup outcomes.

## Limitations and Claim Boundary

Imaging labels, demographic groups, and fairness metrics differ from glucose-event EO and MIT-BIH classification.

## Relevance to This Article

Keep as a clinical fair-KD precedent, without treating it as evidence for Time-LLM or forecasting.
