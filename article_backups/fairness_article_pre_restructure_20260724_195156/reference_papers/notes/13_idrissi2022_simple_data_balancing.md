## Why It Matters

Evidence that simple data balancing can be competitive for worst-group accuracy, directly relevant to the teacher-side exposure-balancing rationale.

## Evidence Scope

This note records claims at the scope supported by the locally archived source or its verified metadata. It is a literature-tracking record, not a source of quantitative claims beyond the cited paper.

## Research Question

Whether simple balancing can match more complex methods for worst-group performance.

## Method and Evidence

Compares data-balancing strategies with group-robust training methods on group-shift benchmarks.

## Findings Relevant Here

Shows that sampling and data exposure can materially affect subgroup performance.

## Limitations and Claim Boundary

Worst-group accuracy and benchmark data shifts are different from demographic EO in glucose forecasting.

## Relevance to This Article

Keep as motivation for EBTD, while avoiding any claim that balancing guarantees fairness.
