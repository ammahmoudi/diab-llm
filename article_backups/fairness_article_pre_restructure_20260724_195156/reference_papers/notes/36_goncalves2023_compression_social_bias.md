## Why It Matters

A controlled study of social-bias effects under LLM quantization and KD; informative but peripheral to clinical Time-LLM fairness.

## Evidence Scope

## Research Question

How quantization and knowledge distillation affect social-bias measures in large language models.

## Method and Evidence

Compares compression methods and language-model characteristics using social-bias and quality evaluations.

## Findings Relevant Here

Finds that compression effects are metric and method dependent, with potential quality trade-offs.

## Limitations and Claim Boundary

The social-bias benchmarks and generative LLM setting are not clinical numerical forecasting or event-level EO.
