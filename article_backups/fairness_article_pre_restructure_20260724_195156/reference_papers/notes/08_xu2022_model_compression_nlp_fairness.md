## Why It Matters

A direct study of social-bias effects under NLP model compression, useful only for the qualified statement that compression effects are benchmark dependent.

## Evidence Scope

This note records claims at the scope supported by the locally archived source or its verified metadata. It is a literature-tracking record, not a source of quantitative claims beyond the cited paper.

## Research Question

Whether model compression can improve measured NLP fairness.

## Method and Evidence

Evaluates compressed NLP models with social-bias benchmarks and task-quality measures.

## Findings Relevant Here

Reports that selected compression choices can reduce some measured bias, with outcomes depending on model and benchmark.

## Limitations and Claim Boundary

NLP bias benchmarks are not clinical fairness endpoints and cannot establish effects in CGM or ECG tasks.
