## Why It Matters

Clinical consensus grounding the 70 mg/dL hypoglycemia threshold used for the primary event endpoint.

## Evidence Scope

This note records claims at the scope supported by the locally archived source or its verified metadata. It is a literature-tracking record, not a source of quantitative claims beyond the cited paper.

## Research Question

What CGM targets should guide interpretation of time in range and time below range.

## Method and Evidence

International consensus recommendations for CGM data interpretation.

## Findings Relevant Here

Defines level-1 hypoglycemia/time-below-range at 70 mg/dL, supporting the clinical threshold rather than a fairness claim.

## Limitations and Claim Boundary

It does not prescribe a model metric or demographic fairness criterion.

## Relevance to This Article

Keep; it is essential clinical grounding for the primary event definition.
