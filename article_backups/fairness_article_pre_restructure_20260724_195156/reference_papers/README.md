## Verification Notes

- Every retained PDF was checked for a PDF header, extractable text, and title match.
- Publisher fallback files for Wolff et al. and Kaur et al. were false matches and were deleted; those records remain abstract/metadata-only.
- The direct OhioT1DM KD papers report aggregate prediction metrics but no protected-group fairness endpoint.
- The direct ECG disparity papers do not use MIT-BIH or knowledge distillation.
- `What is Left After Distillation?` is peer-reviewed in TMLR; `Fair Distillation` and the two newly added compression-fairness papers remain preprints.
